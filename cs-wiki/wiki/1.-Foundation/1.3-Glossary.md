

_Standard definitions for systems, workflow terms, and MindX-specific jargon._

*Goal: To ensure clear communication between the Help Desk, Development Team, and Management by standardizing terminology.*

## 1. Systems & Platforms
- **LMS (Learning Management System)**: The primary student-facing platform used for accessing course materials, submitting assignments, and tracking learning progress.
- **CRM (Customer Relationship Management)**: The internal administrative system used to manage student profiles, financial records (tuition), and enrollment status.
- **Odoo**: The centralized platform for ticket management, operational reporting, and internal documentation.

## 2. CRM Concepts
- **Customer (Khách hàng)**: Người được salesman chăm sóc, tư vấn; là người trả học phí
- **Family member (Thành viên)**: Người có nhu cầu dùng sản phẩm Xschool, có thể không trả tiền
- **Candidate (Học viên)**: Người tham gia học tại Xschool
- **Contact (Liên hệ)**: Đại diện một người trên CRM, định danh bằng ID; customer và family member đều là contact
- **Status (Trạng thái)**: Các giai đoạn chăm sóc KH tiềm năng → thành học viên
- **Course (Khóa học)**: Hệ thống khóa học đang giảng dạy tại Xschool
- **Product (Sản phẩm)**: Khóa học gắn với giá tiền, do MindX cung cấp

## 3. Ticket Statuses (Odoo Workflow)

| Status | Meaning | Who Can Change |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New** | Ticket just created, not yet assigned. | System / Auto |
| **In Progress** | Agent is actively investigating or resolving. | Agent |
| **On Hold** | Waiting for information from BU or student. | Agent |
| **Pending Escalation** | Transferred to Tier 2/3, awaiting response. | Agent / Senior |
| **Resolved** | Fix applied, waiting for student confirmation. | Agent |
| **Closed** | Student confirmed or 48h silent passed. | Agent / System |

## 3. Role & Team Terms
- **BU (Business Unit)**: Internal requesters — Sales, Ops, Academic teams who file tickets on behalf of students.
- **PO (Product Owner)**: Owns a specific system feature; decides on feature changes and prioritization.
- **DPO (Data Protection Officer)**: Responsible for privacy compliance and data breach handling.
- **Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3**: Support levels — T1 handles standard diagnosis, T2 (Senior) handles complex logic, T3 (Dev) handles code fixes.
- **Agent**: Help Desk staff member handling tickets.
- **Senior Agent**: Experienced agent with authority to make override decisions and approve workarounds.

## 4. Service & Workflow Terms
- **SLA (Service Level Agreement)**: A formal commitment defining the maximum time allowed for response and resolution based on a ticket's priority.
- **Triage**: The initial process of analyzing, categorizing (LMS vs. CRM), and prioritizing (Expedite vs. Standard) incoming support tickets.
- **Ack (Acknowledgment)**: The critical first response sent to a student to confirm their issue is received and being investigated (Step 2 of the Workflow).
- **Escalation**: The process of transferring a ticket to a higher technical or management tier when the initial agent cannot resolve the issue.
- **Handover**: The structured transfer of a ticket from Help Desk to Dev team, including all diagnostic evidence.
- **Workaround**: A temporary fix that restores the student's ability to learn while the permanent backend fix is being developed.
- **Resolution**: The final state of a ticket once the fix is verified and the customer has been notified of the outcome.

## 5. Priority Levels
- **P0 (Expedite)**: System-wide outage affecting 25+ users. Immediate response required.
- **P1 (Priority)**: Feature broken for a group. Urgent but not catastrophic.
- **P2 (Standard)**: Individual issue. Normal queue processing.
- **P3 (Low)**: Cosmetic, nice-to-have, or informational request.

## 6. MindX Jargon & Product Terms
- **Enrollment**: The operational process of assigning a student to a specific class and unlocking their access to the corresponding curriculum on the LMS.
- **Slot**: A specific learning module, lesson block, or time interval displayed on the student's LMS dashboard.
- **Checkpoint**: Formal evaluations or tests conducted at specific milestones in a student's learning journey.
- **Track (Course Track)**: A specific educational path at MindX (e.g., AI4L, Code for Kids).
- **Hub (Offline Center)**: A physical MindX location where students attend classes and access local infrastructure.
- **Preservation (Bảo lưu)**: A formal pause of a student's enrollment, preserving their slot and progress for a defined period.
- **PII (Personally Identifiable Information)**: Student data such as name, phone, email, and ID that must be protected under Decree 13.
- **LDP (Landing Page)**: External marketing page where students register interest; data flows into CRM via UTM parameters.
- **UTM**: Tracking parameters (channel, source, campaign, medium, content) appended to LDP URLs to identify lead origin.
- **Lead**: A potential customer recorded in CRM, tracked through stages L1→L6.
- **Lead Stages (L1–L6)**: L1=New, L2=Contacted, L3=Interested, L4=Negotiation, L5A=Waiting Payment, L5BC=Payment Processing, L6ACX=Closed Won/Lost.
- **Lead Owner**: The salesman responsible for a specific Lead.
- **Lead View**: Permission level that controls which Leads a user can see (own vs. team vs. all).
- **Order**: A product/service purchase record within a Lead; one Lead can have multiple Orders.
- **Upsale**: Upgrading a student from a lower-tier course (e.g., 12T → 24T).
- **Resale**: Re-selling an existing product with adjusted pricing.
- **Payment Allocation**: Distributing a payment across multiple order items.
- **Renew Rule**: Logic that determines when old Leads are released back to the shared pool for reassignment.
- **Churn**: A student who stops attending without formal cancellation.
- **Return**: A former student who re-enrolls after a break.
- **OH (Office Hour)**: Extra help sessions outside regular class time (makeup or tutoring).
- **Makeup Session**: A compensatory class for a student who missed a regular session.
- **Onhold**: A student who temporarily pauses their studies while remaining enrolled.
- **Dropout**: A student who discontinues their course before completion.
- **Retention Confirmation**: Recording a student's intent to continue to the next course level.
- **Enrolled Slot**: A slot where the student has been assigned to a specific class.
- **QR Order**: QR code generated for a specific order — student scans and pays.
- **QR User/Center**: QR code linked to a salesman account or center — reusable for multiple orders.
- **Econtract**: Electronic contract system for creating, sending, and signing agreements digitally.
- **Class Site**: A specific physical classroom location used for attendance filtering on LMS.

---

*Note: Consistent terminology prevents miscommunication across technical and academic departments. Update this glossary when new products, systems, or workflows are introduced.*
