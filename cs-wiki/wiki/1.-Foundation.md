# Foundation

Core knowledge base for the Customer Service team.

*Goal: To establish a unified knowledge base of core systems, service standards, and technical terminology for the MindX Help Desk team.*

  

## **Section Overview**

This section provides the SSOT (Single Source of Truth) for every support agent. It ensures a deep understanding of our operational platforms and the service commitments we make to our students and partners.

  

## **Core Principles**

- **System Proficiency**: Agents must know the specific functions of CRM and LMS to diagnose issues accurately.

- **Metric Compliance**: Adhering to the SLA is mandatory to ensure predictable and professional response times.

- **Terminology Synchronization**: Using standardized terms from the Glossary prevents miscommunication across technical and academic departments.

- **Knowledge Validation**: Knowledge is validated through use, not by experts. All articles must be tested against real tickets.

  

## **Key Sub-sections**

- **[1.1 Products & Services](1.-Foundation/CRM/v1/1.1-Products-&-Services)**: Read this first to understand CRM vs. LMS responsibilities and common ticket categories. Refer back when categorizing new tickets.

- **[1.2 Service Tiers & SLAs](1.-Foundation/1.2-Service-Tiers-&-SLAs)**: Use to determine priority level (P0-P3) and calculate response/resolution deadlines. Check before committing a timeline to the student.

- **[1.3 Glossary](1.-Foundation/1.3-Glossary)**: Look up any term you don't recognize in tickets or templates. Standardize terminology across teams.

- **[1.4 Tools & Access](1.-Foundation/1.4-Tools-&-Access)**: Use to request permissions for systems you can't access. Follow the exact escalation path for missing tools.
