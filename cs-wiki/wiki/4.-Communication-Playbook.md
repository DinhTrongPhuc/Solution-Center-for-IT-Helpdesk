


# Communication Playbook

*Goal: To define the "MindX Tech Voice"—a blend of technical expertise, professional etiquette, and friendly support.*

  

## **Section Overview**

Communication is a core part of our product. This section provides the frameworks and personas needed to maintain high-quality interactions with students and internal stakeholders.

  

## **Core Principles**

- **Consistent Persona**: Adopting the "Expert Friend" tone ensures we are perceived as both knowledgeable and approachable.

- **Structured Briefing**: Using models like ISA (Impact-SLA-Action) ensures management receives concise and actionable data.

- **Hierarchy & Respect**: Standardized Vietnamese addressing (e.g., Em/Anh/Chi) maintains professional discipline within the organization.

  

## **Key Sub-sections**

- **[4.1 To Customers](4.-Communication-Playbook/4.1-To-Customers)**: Use "Expert Friend" tone — professional, clear, friendly. Include student name, ticket ID, and specific next steps. Avoid jargon. Write in English for international students, Vietnamese for local BUs.

- **[4.2 To Dev Team](4.-Communication-Playbook/4.2-To-Dev-Team)**: Report facts only — system, steps to reproduce, error message, expected vs actual behavior. Do NOT include opinions or student complaints. Use screenshots and exact timestamps.

- **[4.3 To Management](4.-Communication-Playbook/4.3-To-Management)**: Use ISA format (Impact → SLA → Action). State the business impact first, then SLA breach risk, then what you need from them. Keep to 3-5 bullet points.
