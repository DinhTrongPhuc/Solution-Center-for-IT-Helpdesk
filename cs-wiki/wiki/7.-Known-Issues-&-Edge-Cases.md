# Known Issues & Edge Cases

Documented bugs, workarounds, and edge cases.


*Goal: To maintain a dynamic repository of recurring technical issues and non-standard scenarios to ensure consistent decision-making and rapid resolution.*

  

## **Section Overview**

This section serves as the "Operational Memory" of the MindX Help Desk. By documenting verified bugs and strategic exceptions, we empower agents to handle complex situations based on historical precedents rather than guesswork.

  

## **Core Principles**

- **Collective Memory**: Converting individual ticket resolutions into shared organizational knowledge.

- **Operational Agility**: Prioritizing immediate student access via workarounds while permanent fixes are pending.

- **Precedent Logic**: Ensuring fairness by handling rare scenarios with the same logic used in the past.

  

## **Key Sub-sections**

- **[7.1 Known Bugs & Workarounds](7.-Known-Issues-&-Edge-Cases/7.1-Known-Bugs-&-Workarounds)**: Catalog of recurring technical issues and their step-by-step temporary fixes.

- **[7.2 Edge Cases](7.-Known-Issues-&-Edge-Cases/7.2-Edge-Cases)**: Documentation of high-complexity scenarios and approved management rulings.

- **[7.3 HTML Templates Archive](7.-Known-Issues-&-Edge-Cases/7.3-HTML-Templates-Archive)**: To provide a structured repository of standardized HTML response templates for consistent communication and rapid incident resolution.
