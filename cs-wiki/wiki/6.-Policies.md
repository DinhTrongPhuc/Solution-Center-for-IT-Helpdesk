# Policies

Operational policies governing CS interactions.

*Goal: To establish a comprehensive legal and operational framework that protects student data and system integrity.*

  

## **Section Overview**

This section serves as the "Rules of Engagement" for MindX support staff. These policies are mandatory to maintain compliance with Vietnamese law and protect company digital assets.

  

## **Core Principles**

- **Legal Compliance**: All data handling must align with the Law on Cybersecurity and Decree 13/2023/ND-CP.

- **Individual Accountability**: Every action on internal systems must be traceable to a verified employee identity.

- **Data Privacy First**: Protecting student PII is a non-negotiable legal and ethical obligation.

  

## **Key Sub-sections**

- **[6.1 Account Management](6.-Policies/6.1-Account-Management)**: Follow to create, modify, or deactivate user accounts. Verify identity before any change. All actions must be logged.

- **[6.2 Privacy & Data Handling](6.-Policies/6.2-Privacy-&-Data-Handling)**: Follow for data export requests, classification, and security breach reporting. Never share student PII outside approved channels.

  

---

*Note: Compliance protects both the organization and the individual employee from legal risks.*
